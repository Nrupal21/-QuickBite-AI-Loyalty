# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary: independent Indian restaurant owners (Marco, 42, two restaurants in Pune — moderate tech level, lives in WhatsApp/Google Maps, no time for reputation management). Secondary: multi-location restaurant managers (high tech level, want brand-voice consistency and live loyalty data). The landing page speaks to owners; the product's end users are diners (Ananya, 27 — scans a QR, never downloads an app).

## Product Purpose
QuickBite AI + Loyalty is a multi-tenant SaaS that turns one QR code on a restaurant receipt into two engines: an AI review composer (diner scans → stars + tags → GPT-4o drafts the review → pasted to Google in under 30 seconds) and an app-less loyalty stamp card (GPS-geofenced scan → OTP login by SMS, no password, no app). Success: more 5-star reviews and measurable repeat visits with zero manual owner effort.

## Positioning
The only tool where one QR code gets a restaurant both a 5-star review and a returning customer — no app download, no password, no staff effort.

## Conversion & proof
- Primary CTA: "Start Free" → owner registration (Starter plan, free).
- Secondary CTA: "See how it works" → scroll to the 3-step story.
- The line a visitor remembers: "Turn every meal into a 5-star story."
- Belief ladder: (1) reviews and repeat customers are won or lost at the table → (2) diners will act if it takes 30 seconds and no app → (3) AI removes the owner's effort entirely → (4) it's free to start.
- Proof on hand: none yet (pre-launch). Use the product itself as proof — live loyalty-card and review-draft mockups, not testimonials.

## Brand Personality
Energetic, trustworthy, appetising. Feels like a modern Indian food-tech brand (Zomato/Swiggy energy) with SaaS credibility — never corporate-sterile, never toy-like. Warmth comes from the orange accent and food imagery; trust from the blue; the loyalty teal is the product's signature moment.

## Anti-references
- Generic AI-SaaS landing pages: cream backgrounds, gradient text, identical icon-card grids, uppercase eyebrow labels over every section.
- Corporate enterprise-software pages (grey, dense, jargon).
- Editorial-magazine aesthetics — this is a food-tech product page, not a journal.

## Design Principles
1. Show the product, don't describe it — the loyalty card, stamp grid, and AI draft appear as working mockups on the page.
2. One idea per fold — long scroll, deliberate pacing, scroll-driven storytelling (Awwwards scrolling-gallery reference).
3. The teal moment is earned — the page starts white/blue and *arrives* at the drenched teal loyalty section, mirroring the diner's journey to a stamp.
4. Committed color, exact hexes — the Doc 4 palette (11 colors) is law; teal #0D9488 carries loyalty, orange #FF6B35 converts, blue #1A56DB anchors.
5. Motion serves narrative — GSAP scroll reveals and the Three.js hero are the story's pacing, with a full prefers-reduced-motion fallback.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
prefers-reduced-motion support is mandatory on every animation (Doc 4 §7.8). Body text ≥ 4.5:1 contrast; large white text on teal only at ≥18px bold. Touch targets ≥ 44px. `inputmode` attributes on numeric/tel inputs. Works on 375px viewports — diners are mobile-only.
